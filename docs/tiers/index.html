Roofoo Star Tiers

What Roofoo's filter stars, and the skill weapons it highlights.

Filter Builder

Unidentified uniques and set items get a star tier by the base they drop on. The filter can't tell which unique a base is until it's identified, so every item on a base shares that base's tier. Ethereal copies have their own tier.

These lists are the same in Roofoo, Roofoo Mystery and Roofoo Mystery (BETA). The staff mod bases are in the BETA filter.

    Changing a tier

    1. Open the sheet on GitHub and click the pencil to edit.
    2. Change Normal or Ethereal to 3 Star Pick-up, 3 Star, 1 Star, No Star or Default. For a staff mod, set Show to No to turn it off, or edit its Note (| starts a new line).
    3. Commit the change. Rows not in the filter yet are marked pending here until the filter is rebuilt.

    Without edit access, GitHub saves your change as a suggestion (a pull request) for Roofoo to approve.
